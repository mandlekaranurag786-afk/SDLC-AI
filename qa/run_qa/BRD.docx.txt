Executive SummaryThe current employee onboarding process is fragmented, involving multiple departments such as HR, IT, and reporting managers, leading to inefficiencies and a lack of accountability. The objective of this project is to streamline and automate the onboarding process, thereby reducing manual effort and improving overall efficiency. The expected outcome is a cohesive onboarding experience that enhances visibility, accountability, and satisfaction for new employees while minimizing delays and errors in asset provisioning and access management.2. Business ProblemThe existing onboarding process is characterized by scattered coordination among HR, IT, and reporting managers, resulting in significant manual effort and inefficiencies. Key pain points include manual and repetitive coordination, delays in asset and access provisioning, scattered meeting scheduling, lack of real-time visibility, inconsistent candidate experiences, and absence of accountability or audit trails. These issues necessitate a comprehensive solution to enhance the onboarding experience for new employees and streamline operations across departments.3. Functional OverviewThe system will provide an automated onboarding checklist that assigns tasks to relevant stakeholders, ensuring timely completion. Automated reminders will be sent to HR, IT, and reporting managers to keep them informed of pending tasks. Document management features will allow for easy upload, verification, and e-signature of necessary documents. The onboarding status will be trackable in real-time, and IT will receive automated requests for asset and access provisioning. Meeting scheduling will be integrated with existing calendar systems, while an AI-powered assistant will be available for new employees to address their queries. The system will also track SLAs and provide analytics and reports on the onboarding process.7.
